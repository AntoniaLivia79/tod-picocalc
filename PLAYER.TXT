========================================
      TUNNELS OF DOOM - PLAYER GUIDE
========================================

GETTING STARTED
---------------
1. RUN "TOD.BAS". On the title screen:
   N = new quest, C = continue saved,
   Q = quit.
2. Pick an adventure (.ADV file).
3. ENTER sets out with your last party,
   N makes a new one: 1-4 adventurers,
   R re-rolls stats, ENTER keeps them.
4. Choose a difficulty: 1 easy to 3
   hard.
5. Buy gear in the store, then press
   ESC to enter the dungeon.

CLASSES
-------
Each adventure names the four classes
its own way (in Moria: Ranger, Wizard,
Hobbit, Elf). In order they are:

1 Fighter  Most hit points, best with
           weapons and armor.
2 Wizard   Fewest hit points, but can
           read magic scrolls.
3 Rogue    Light armor, good at
           avoiding traps.
4 Hero     Can do it all, but only in
           a one-player party.

GOAL
----
Find every quest object, then climb
the up-stairs on floor 1 to win.
Some objects have a time limit!
Press 2 to see the turns left
(1 turn = 10 steps).

EXPLORING
---------
Rooms:    arrows move out an exit.
Hallways: UP walks forward,
          LEFT/RIGHT turn,
          DOWN turns around.

>  or .   go down stairs
<  or ,   go up stairs
M         floor map
1         player report (then M for
          magic item details)
2         party report
U         use magic item
T         trade or drop magic item
O         change who leads
L         listen at a door
D         drink at a hallway fountain
K         save game (hallway only)
Q         abandon quest
?  or H   command help

You may need a floor's map before you
can go down. Maps hide in rooms.

COMBAT
------
Each player may take one step, then
attack. The highlighted name shows
whose turn it is.

Arrow     step, or hit adjacent foe
F         fire ranged weapon; aim
          (a sling never runs out)
          You see the shot fly.
          with arrow keys, SPACE fires
W         switch weapon
N         negotiate: Y pay,
          N haggle, R refuse
U         use magic item
ENTER     end this player's turn

HP = hit points, WD = wounds taken.
When WD reaches HP the player falls.
++ + 0 - -- shows your odds in a
fight: ++ is best, -- is worst.

A player can carry two weapons. If
both are full you are shown them both
and asked which to replace. ESC keeps
what you have.

ROOMS
-----
Chest     May be trapped.
Vault     Guess 3 digits. You are
          told how many are right
          and HIGHER or LOWER.
Fountain  One drink each. Good or
          bad luck! Some stand in the
          hallways: press D to drink.
Statue    Pay gold to identify a
          magic item. Pay too
          little and it may break.
Store     Buy more supplies. Use < and
          > to turn the page.

STAYING ALIVE
-------------
- Every 10 steps the party eats one
  ration and heals one wound each.
  No rations means no healing.
- Healing packs heal one wound per
  step until used up.
- Unknown magic may be cursed. Try
  it when you are safe.
- Negotiating can save a weak party.

SAVING
------
Press K in a HALLWAY to save. You
cannot save inside a room - step out
into the hallway first.
Saved to TODSAVE.DAT.
There is one save slot. Choose C on
the title screen to continue. Keep
the .ADV file on the card.

Good luck, adventurers!
